1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites work, remember choices, improve performance and understand how visitors use pages.

2. How we use cookies

The Bodelwyddan Residential Care website may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the website working, remember your cookie choices, understand website performance and help us improve information for families searching for residential care in Rhosneigr, Anglesey and North Wales.

3. Types of cookies we may use

Essential

Required for basic website operation, security and remembering cookie preferences.

Analytics

Used to understand page visits, traffic sources and how visitors interact with the website.

Tag management

Google Tag Manager helps manage scripts such as analytics tags in one place.

Search Console

Google Search Console helps us monitor search visibility, indexing and search performance.

4. Cookies and tools table

Tool or cookie Purpose Type
Cookie preference storage Remembers whether you accepted or rejected optional cookies, so the banner does not need to ask repeatedly. Essential

5. Consent for analytics cookies

Where analytics cookies are not strictly necessary, we will ask for your consent before using them. You can accept or reject optional cookies through the website cookie banner, and you can also manage cookies through your browser settings.

6. Third-party services

Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Search Console are provided by Google. These tools may process technical information about website use. You can learn more about Google privacy controls through Google’s own privacy and advertising settings.

7. Managing cookies in your browser

You can usually block, delete or manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how parts of the website work, especially cookie preference tools or form-related features.
